Chapter 03, The process

How I work

Four simple steps. Your team always knows what is happening.

  1. Listen

    I talk to users and to your team. I want to know the real problem before I draw anything.

  2. Sketch

    I sketch the flow and make quick wireframes. Mistakes are cheap to fix at this stage.

  3. Design

    I design clean screens in Figma and build a design system your team can reuse.

  4. Build

    I hand over clean code, or I code it myself. What you approve is what goes live.

Chapter 04, The proof

Work I'm proud of

Every project began with a real problem. Here is what was wrong, what I did, and what changed.

Case 01, Mobile app, personal finance

Monity

The problem
Most people quit budgeting apps within two weeks. Typing in every receipt gets boring fast.
What I did
I added receipt scanning, put the debt goal right on the home screen, and built in an AI helper that gives simple tips.
The result
A friendly app that makes saving money feel doable.
Case 02, Mobile app, trading

Traders Hub

The problem
Traders were jumping between many apps, and that led to slow, stressed decisions.
What I did
I put the live market, a calendar, daily levels and coach chat on one dashboard, in a dark theme that is easy on the eyes.
The result
One place for the whole trading day.
Case 03, Mobile app, solar energy

Solar 4 Good

The problem
Customers, sales reps, survey teams and admins all worked in separate chats and spreadsheets.
What I did
I designed four role-based flows inside one app. Customers see simple energy cards. Field teams see a clear task list.
The result
Fewer support calls and faster handoffs between teams.
Case 04, Web platform, online learning

Young Rocket

The problem
Video course sites feel crowded, and free and paid lessons look the same.
What I did
I designed a clean video page with clear Free and Premium tags, and I coded it myself in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
The result
Students can focus on the lesson, and the finished page matches the design.
