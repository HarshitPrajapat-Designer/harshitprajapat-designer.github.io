Auraa Lifestyle — Fashion & Lifestyle Exhibition

1. Project Overview

Brand: Auraa Lifestyle

Role: Visual & Social Media Designer

Timeline: 3 Months

Tools Used: Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

2. The Challenge

Auraa Lifestyle needed to build pre-event awareness, drive footfall for their multi-day exhibition, and establish a consistent luxury visual identity across digital channels.

Their social media needed to reflect two things simultaneously:

Regional Cultural Elegance: Connecting with the heritage and royal identity of Jodhpur and surrounding regions.

Contemporary Fashion Appeal: Attracting modern, fashion-forward shoppers and independent boutique clothing labels.

3. Visual Strategy & Creative Direction

Warm, Editorial Palette: Used soft pastel tones, subtle gold, and warm peach/beige backdrops to give an editorial, high-end magazine look.

Heritage Meets Modern Fashion: Blended iconic architectural silhouettes (such as Mehrangarh / Jodhpur heritage motifs) with modern cutout fashion photography to root the event locally while keeping it aspirational.

Structured 9-Grid Layout: Designed cohesive multi-post grid layouts for Instagram so the profile looks intentional, organized, and premium when a prospective attendee lands on the feed.

4. Key Deliverables & Social Media Execution

Event Announcement Posts: Teaser banners communicating dates, venue, and partner affiliations clearly without cluttering the frame.

Curated Grid Carousels & Lookbooks: Highlighting featured clothing categories, designer labels, and curated collections using minimal typography and balanced negative space.

Partner & Sponsor Creatives: Clean integration of associate partner branding (e.g., Rotary Club association) maintaining visual hierarchy so promotional elements never overpower the core aesthetic.

5. Impact & Key Takeaways

Consistent Visual Identity: Replaced random, disjointed promotional graphics with a unified visual language across all 3 months of the event campaign.

Higher Engagement & Clarity: Structured typography and clear hierarchy reduced visitor confusion regarding event dates and stall booking details.

Brand Recall: Established a distinct warm-editorial tone that distinguished Auraa from generic exhibition advertisements in the regional market.

Solar 4 Good — Multi-Role Solar Energy Management & Field Operations App

1. Project Overview

Project Name: Solar 4 Good (S4G)

Role: Lead UI/UX Designer

Timeline: 1 – 1.5 Months

Platform: Mobile Application (iOS & Android)

Tools Used: Figma, FigJam, Adobe Illustrator

Deliverables: User Persona Mapping, Role-Based Information Architecture, Wireframes, Interactive Components, and Production-Ready UI Design Systems.

2. The Core Problem & Context

The residential and commercial solar installation lifecycle involves multiple stakeholders who often work in silos: homeowners want clarity on energy savings, sales reps chase pipeline updates, survey teams need on-site inspection clarity, and operations admins handle customer escalations.

Prior to this solution, teams relied on fragmented communication channels (phone calls, spreadsheets, third-party messaging), leading to:

High cognitive load for customers trying to interpret technical kilowatt-hour and battery discharge metrics.

Delayed site surveys due to lack of real-time project milestone tracking.

Repetitive customer service queries regarding installation progress and hardware performance.

3. UX Strategy: Role-Based Architecture

To keep the app intuitive without overwhelming any single group, the user experience was decoupled into four distinct role flows right from login:

I. Customer Portal (Simplicity & Transparency):

  • UX Focus: Converting complex electrical data into digestible, human-friendly cards.
  • Design Execution: Highlighted "Solar Energy Produced" ($1.8\text{ kW}$) alongside real-time grid consumption ($175.0\text{ kWh}$) and live battery percentage ($50\%$). A visual timeline card keeps homeowners updated on upcoming site visits and installation phases without having to call support.

II. Sales Executive Flow (Pipeline Velocity):

  • UX Focus: Glanceable metrics for fast decision-making on the go.
  • Design Execution: Top-level metric cards separating Total Projects, Completed Installations, Open Leads, and Pending Surveys, enabling reps to follow up with leads in seconds.

III. Survey & Installation Flow (Task Execution):

  • UX Focus: Clear operational status for field technicians.
  • Design Execution: Quick priority tags indicating assigned site visits, pending hardware installations, open support tickets, and direct customer contact shortcuts.

IV. S4G Admin Hub (Team Coordination & Support):

  • UX Focus: Fast triage and resolution of customer inquiries.
  • Design Execution:Centralized message queues and categorized support tickets (e.g., App Issue, Battery Hardware Issue) with immediate assignment toggles.

4. UI System & Visual Design Choices

Color Psychology (Solar Energy Tones):
Selected an energetic, warm amber/yellow primary hue (#FFB800 range) balanced with clean white cards and soft warm grays. This instantly evokes clean solar energy while maintaining high contrast for outdoor field use.

Component Modularity:
Standardized rounded card containers (border-radius: 16px - 20px), soft drop shadows, and clean pill badges to maintain visual hierarchy across all four dashboard views.

Thumb-Zone Navigation:
Designed ergonomic bottom navigation bars tailored to each role, placing frequent actions (Home, Projects, Customer Lists, Chat, Tickets) within natural thumb reach for one-handed outdoor usage.

Clear Data Chunking:
Broke down dense telemetry (Battery, Power, Energy tabs) into segmented controls, preventing screen clutter and reducing visual fatigue.

5. Key UX Deliverables & Flow Validation

Frictionless Role Selector: A card-based authentication gate allowing users to pick their operational category cleanly before entering credentials.

In-App Issue Reporting: A 2-tap support ticket flow directly accessible from the customer dashboard to report hardware/inverter issues without calling external helplines.

Unified Design Tokens: Maintained typography scales, iconography sets, and input fields across all 4 dashboards, ensuring seamless engineering handoff and low development overhead.

6. Results & Impact

Unified Operational Ecosystem: Successfully replaced multiple disconnected tracking spreadsheets with a single, role-responsive mobile platform.

Reduced Inbound Support Burden: Giving customers direct visibility into their installation progress timeline and live solar generation cut recurring support calls significantly.

Faster Field Handoffs: Clear task assignment cards streamlined communication between sales closers and on-site technical survey crews.
